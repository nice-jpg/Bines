# Menu collection
Assign alias "1" to the right-hand vertical product list beneath the appropriate-ordering reminder, beside the category rail. Retain this list anchor when the reminder scrolls away. Exclude merchant headers, category rail, checkout bar, reminder, promotional banners and section headings.

Collect name, current_price, original_price, monthly_sales in visible item order. Purchasable cards, including live-exclusive and subsidized offers, have a product title, image, price and add/specification/select-meal control. Promotional prose without such a card is not an item. Titles may wrap; combine title fragments inside their card. Monthly sales starts with 月售. Join split currency and numeric price fragments and attached 起; ignore savings and offer badges. Original price is the same-card crossed-out list price. Missing optional fields use empty strings. Require a complete readable title and current price within the visible menu viewport; exclude cards clipped before those fields are readable, even if their hidden text exists.

Locate fields relative to each repeated card, never from other cards or historical states. Do not confuse a banner advertising a price with a purchasable card. Rewrite this guide only when layout or requested schema changes, not for merchant names, product labels, IDs or coordinates.
